# AI-enabled product development

Mattias uses AI as a product-building capability: combining models, APIs and agentic tools to explore ideas, redesign workflows and build products that a small team can realistically take from concept to working experience. His hands-on work complements 10+ years of leading product and digital transformation in complex organisations: the goal is to understand what new capabilities mean in practice, then translate them into useful products and workflows. His independent projects include SongAtlas, BikerBrain (formerly BikeMaster), Wavemap (formerly Hip-hop Atlas), StoryTailor and this Resume Agent.

## How he approaches AI

- Start with the user problem, then identify where AI can create new capability, speed or creative range.
- Combine models and services according to their strengths rather than treating one model as the answer to every task.
- Prototype quickly, learn from working flows and turn promising experiments into usable product experiences.
- Design human + AI collaboration, review steps and recovery paths into the workflow where they add value.
- Keep important product logic, permissions and sensitive-data boundaries explicit when the context calls for it.
- Treat evaluation, iteration and adoption as product work, not an afterthought.
