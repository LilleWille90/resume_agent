BikeMaster

An advanced connected cycling experience, built by one product builder by combining sensor data, modern APIs and AI-assisted product development.

Problem
Turn repeated rides, GPS, IMU, weather and route signals into useful cycling intelligence.
Role
Founder and product builder across concept, iOS/watchOS experience, technical direction and validation.
AI
AI coding agents and AI-assisted design, testing and documentation compress the path from idea to a working connected product. The product also explores AI-based explanations grounded in curated ride insights.
Learning
AI gives a solo builder more leverage; deterministic product logic and well-defined data boundaries keep the core experience reliable.

End-to-end side project

StoryTailor

A voice recording becomes a personal, illustrated and printable children’s book through an orchestrated, multimodal AI product experience.

Problem
Make it possible to preserve a family story without needing writing, illustration, design or print-production expertise.
Role
Solo product builder, from customer journey and UX through engineering, payments and print fulfilment.
AI
OpenAI, Claude and Replicate image models each handle the work they are best suited to—from transcription and story analysis to prompts and illustration generation.
Learning
Real product innovation lies in the orchestration: handling character consistency, structured-output failures and print constraints so a many-specialist workflow feels like one service.

Resume Agent

An experiment in turning a static CV into an interactive, retrieval-grounded recruiter experience.

Problem

Role
Product owner and builder, evolving a CV chatbot into a conversational portfolio.
AI
Structured knowledge plus an LLM and source grounding lets recruiters explore the portfolio naturally, with answers anchored in the underlying material.
Learning
Even a familiar artifact can become a more useful product when human-curated knowledge and conversational AI work together.
